**Title:** How Many Alien Civilizations Are in Our Galaxy? 👽 #shorts

**Description:**
We plugged real measurements — and some hopeful guesses — into the Drake equation.
The answer raises a much bigger question… The full story is in our documentary: "Are We Alone? The Mystery of the Fermi Paradox."

Values used: R* ≈ 2 · fp ≈ 1 · ne ≈ 0.2 (measured) · fl = 0.5 · fi = 0.1 · fc = 0.1 · L = 1,000,000 years (guesses).
Change the guesses and the answer swings from less than one to millions — that uncertainty is the whole point.
